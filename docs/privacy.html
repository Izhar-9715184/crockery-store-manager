Crockery Store Manager – Privacy Policy

Crockery Store Manager is a desktop program that a shop uses to keep its stock, sales, customers and credit (udhar) records on its own computer.

What the Google sign-in is used for

If the shop owner chooses "Sign in with Google", the program asks for two things only:

  • the shop owner's email address, to show which account is connected;
  • access to the program's own hidden app folder in the owner's Google Drive (the "drive.appdata" permission). The program cannot see, open or change any other file in the owner's Drive.

What is stored

A backup copy of the shop's data file is saved in that hidden folder, so the shop can restore its data on another computer. The sign-in token stays on the shop's own computer.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not have a server. The shop's data goes directly from the shop's computer to the shop's own Google Drive.
  • We never receive, read, sell or share the shop's data or email address.
  • No advertising or analytics.

Stopping and deleting

The owner can press "Sign out" in the program's Settings, or remove the program's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup files can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.
